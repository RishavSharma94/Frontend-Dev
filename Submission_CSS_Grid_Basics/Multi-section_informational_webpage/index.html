Overview

Paris is the capital city of France, known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. It is famous for landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre Museum.

Top Attractions

Some must-visit attractions include the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, Sacré-Cœur, Champs-Élysées, and the Louvre Museum.

Culture & Lifestyle

Paris is known for its cafés, art galleries, and fashion houses. The city has a rich history in art, literature, and music.

Visit Tips

Best time to visit is spring and fall. Always book tickets in advance for popular attractions, and try local pastries and wines.
